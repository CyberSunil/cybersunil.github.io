Relative Motion: Train Problems — Reference Notes

Standard method: head start = speed1 × Δt; relative speed = |v2 − v1|; catch-up time = head start / relative speed.

Example: A departs 14:05 at 80 km/h, B departs 14:20 at 110 km/h. Head start 20 km, relative 30 km/h, catch-up 40 min, meeting 15:00.
